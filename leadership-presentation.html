The status quo we're replacing

What the manual process actually costs

  • Ledgers live in registers or spreadsheets — no one can see today's collection total without asking accounts to compile it.
  • Reminders are ad-hoc — whoever remembers to chase a family does; there's no systematic way to see who's actually overdue longest.
  • Receipts are hand-written or typed per case — numbering, balance math, and reconciliation are all manual and error-prone.
  • There is no trail — if a collection is questioned six months later, there's no record of who collected it, when, or how.
  • Multiple campuses mean multiple spreadsheets — leadership stitches together numbers from each school separately.
Outcome 01 — Fee Recovery

Estimated 3× faster fee recovery cycle

Not a marketing number — a workflow comparison. Three manual steps that used to take minutes now take seconds, and the system tells staff who to chase first instead of leaving it to memory.

Before — manual office

  1. Look up the ledger — flip through a register or spreadsheet to find the balance (2–3 min)
  2. Write the receipt — hand-compute the remaining balance, assign the next number (2–3 min)
  3. Draft a reminder — manually type a WhatsApp/SMS message per family (1–2 min)
  4. Guess who's overdue — no ranking, so the most overdue families often get chased last
→

After — this system

  1. Type-ahead student search auto-loads live pending dues (< 5 sec)
  2. One click collects, auto-numbers the receipt, updates the screen instantly (< 10 sec)
  3. One click opens WhatsApp with the reminder pre-written (~5 sec)
  4. Auto-ranked risk badges put HIGH-risk families at the top, every time
A workflow-derived estimate, not an audited benchmark — validate against your own time-motion data post go-live.
Outcome 01 — Detail

The system tells staff who to chase first

A deterministic risk score (no black-box ML) runs on every pending due, so the pending-dues list surfaces the accounts that matter most instead of a flat, unordered list.

Signal 1 · 50% weight

Days overdue

The clearest signal a due is actually late — capped at 90 days so one extreme outlier can't dominate the score.

Signal 2 · 25% weight

Pending due count

Multiple simultaneous unpaid dues on one student compound the risk of non-recovery.

Signal 3 · 25% weight

Late-payment history

A softer, longer-run signal — how often this family's past receipts landed after the due date.

Score ≥ 55 → High risk (red badge) Score ≥ 25 → Medium risk (amber badge) Below 25 → unbadged, so attention isn't wasted on families already on track
Outcome 01 — Detail

One-click reminders. Instant, flexible receipts.

Live today

WhatsApp reminder, one click

Every pending due row has a reminder button that opens WhatsApp with the parent's number and a pre-written message — student name, fee, amount, due date, school name — already filled in. No drafting, no copy-paste.

Live today

Receipt, generated on collection

Auto-numbered per school (e.g. RCPT-GWS-2026-0042), printable as A4 or an 80mm thermal-counter layout, exportable to PDF in one click — and it shows the family's remaining balance across all other dues, not just this one.

Built-in

Partial payments, handled correctly

Families can pay in installments. The system tracks amount paid vs. total due and only marks a due "settled" once it's actually fully covered.

Ready to activate

Automated WhatsApp receipt confirmation

The exact message payload is already built and logged on every collection — connecting it to Meta's WhatsApp Business API to send automatically is a configuration step, not new development.

Outcome 03 — Financial Integrity

Every rupee has a name, a time, and a reason

Cash handling is where trust breaks down fastest. The system is built so that every collection, every record change, and every settings update leaves a permanent, attributable trail — automatically, not as an afterthought.

On every collection

An immutable receipt record

Every FeeReceipt permanently stores who collected it, the payment mode, and a transaction/cheque reference — UPI and bank transfer payments cannot be recorded without a reference number.

On every mutation

A parallel audit log entry

Fee collections, student changes, attendance edits, and settings updates all write a structured audit event — action type, target record, a human-readable summary, the acting user, and their IP address.

Outcome 03 — Detail

A searchable ledger of every action taken

The Audit Logs screen is filterable by action type and record, searchable by user or detail text, refreshes automatically every 20 seconds, and is visible only to the Director role — leadership sees everything; day-to-day staff see only what their job requires.

Outcome 03 — Detail

Access control that's checked, not assumed

Role-gated by design

Four roles, four different views

Director sees everything including audit logs and settings. Admin runs fees and students. Teacher runs classes and attendance. Parent gets a minimal view. Every restricted page re-checks this on the server — it's not just a hidden menu item.

Session re-verified, every time

No trusting a cookie blindly

Every request re-confirms the signed-in user actually has a live membership at the active school before returning any data — a tampered or stale session cookie resolves to nothing, not someone else's records.

Outcome 04 — Multi-Campus Governance

One login. Every school you run.

Every record in the system — students, fees, attendance, audit logs — hangs off a school as its tenant root. A person can hold different roles at different campuses (Director at one, Teacher at another), and switching schools is a single click.

Data isolation

Campuses can't see each other

Every query is scoped to the active school. A receipt ID from one campus resolves to nothing at another — not an error message revealing it exists, just nothing.

Independent numbering

No collisions across schools

Receipt numbers are namespaced per school (e.g. GWS vs. RIS), so two campuses running the same day never produce a clashing receipt number.

One-click switch

Built for a Director running a group

The school switcher re-verifies real membership before switching — it only appears at all once an account actually has more than one school to switch between.
